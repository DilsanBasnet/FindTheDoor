# FindTheDoor - 2D Game   [![Hackatime](https://img.shields.io/badge/Hackatime-48h_18m-ff477e?logo=wakatime&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/DilsanBasnet)

## DEMO: 

*I Build this 2D game called FindTheDoor. It is a Parkour challenging 2D plaforming game where patience and precision are keys.
Players must navigate very difficult parkour levels & dodging obstacles like; spikes, and wooden-spike-platforms, while collecting collectableItems like coins, diamonds.*

---

*In this game the  player goal is to find the AnyWare Door to escape and to go to next Level.*
*Till now I build upto 1 to 10 handcrafted levels. the levels are very difficult that tests players reflexes and timing. Difficult levels took over 5 hr to build cuz of their difficulty.*


---

*The levels are tough but 100 % beatable with right strategy, timing & patience. Also In background of levels their is a soft bg music.*


## Game Scenes:
- Main menu Scene :- Play and Exit Button, rules, & wooden background 
- level Scene :- gameplay arena till now upto 1 to 10 fully working difficult levels.
- Game Over Scene:- Main menu  & Exit button

## Controls: 
- A - Left move
- D - Right move
- Spacebar - Jump
- Long Press for Long Jump

## Build
- Unity
- C#
- Assets: found after hr of searching on Pinterest
